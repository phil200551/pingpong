How to play

WASDMove around your end of the table — and steer your shot
A/DMoving left/right as you hit sends the ball to the left/right side of their table
W/SMoving forward as you hit = a hard topspin drive that dips and kicks off the table; backward = a soft push
SpaceSwing (left click works too). Timing is everything!
Shift+SpaceBackspin chop: floats low over the net and dies short (right click works too)
EscPause
MMute / unmute all sound
  • Get in line: move so the ball comes to your right (forehand) or left (backhand) within arm's reach.
  • Timing ring: a ring shrinks onto the incoming ball. Swing when it closes and turns green for a PERFECT shot. Red means it's out of reach — move!
  • Better timing = faster, more accurate shots. Sloppy hits scatter and may find the net.
  • SMASH: a perfectly timed hit on a high ball is unstoppable-fast.
  • Aiming: standing still sends the ball straight down the middle at normal pace. Keys combine: W+D is a fast shot to the right. Decent timing always keeps it on the table.
  • Read the spin: the stripe on the ball turns with it. Orange, rolling down towards you = topspin: it dips and kicks forward off the table, so it's on you fast. Blue, rolling up = backspin: it floats, then dies short and low.
  • Spin on your return: topspin coming in kicks a sloppy return long, backspin drags it into the net. Good timing beats both; a chop is the safe answer to heavy backspin.
  • Serving: press Space to toss, then Space again as the ball drops back to about hand height. It must bounce on your side, then theirs.
  • Rules: games to 11, win by 2. Serve switches every 2 points (every point at 10–10). A serve that clips the net and lands in is a let and is replayed.
